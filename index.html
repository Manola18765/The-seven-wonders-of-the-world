The seven wonders of the world


-----------

Since the beginning of the world, There has been a lot of wonders and great architectures through the milleniums of its existence. Below, we will dive through them and I will show them with some details.

  • Great wall of China(China)

    • A massive wall built over 2,000 years ago across northern China to stop attacks.

  • Petra(Jordan)

    • An old city carved right into pink stone cliffs by the Nabataeans, famous for its rock-cut architecture.

  • Colosseum(Italy)

    • A huge ancient open-air arena in Rome used for public shows and gladiator fights.

  • Chichén Itzá(Mexico)

    • A historic Mayan city featuring the large El Castillo stepped pyramid.

  • Machu Picchu(Peru)

    • A famous 15th-century Inca city high up in the Andes mountains.

  • Taj Mahal(India)

    • A stunning white marble mausoleum built by a heartbroken emperor for his wife.

  • Christ the Redeemer(Brazil)

    • A giant Art Deco statue of Jesus Christ looking over Rio de Janeiro from Corcovado mountain.
